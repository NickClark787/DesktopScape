# Colosseum arena renderer (`renderer/colosseum/render`)

The visual layer for the Sol Heredit fight. It is a **rendering-only**
module: it reads engine state and draws it. No tick timing, hazard shape,
attack pattern or RNG lives here — all of that is in
[`src/sim/solHeredit`](../../../sim/solHeredit/README.md), and the
renderer's job is to never disagree with it.

## All art is original

Every shape on the canvas is generated by the code in this directory:
the arena floor and wall, the four corner columns, the spectator
silhouettes, Sol himself, the player marker, prayer glyphs, hitsplats and
the HUD. **No Jagex/OSRS sprite, model, texture, icon or font is copied,
traced, extracted or reproduced**, and none is loaded at runtime. Sol is
an original silhouette assembled from primitives — a plated torso
hexagon, an eight-ray sun emblem, pauldron arcs, a crested helm, a tower
shield and a spear — chosen to convey mass, facing and weapon state, not
to resemble the real model. Fidelity here means *mechanical and
positional* accuracy: tile positions, hazard shapes and telegraph timing
are exact, the appearance is our own.

Gear and item icons elsewhere in DesktopScape are unchanged; this module
adds none.

## Why the engine grew a bigger snapshot

The one rule that keeps the fight honest: **if a visual needs data, the
engine exposes it read-only — the renderer never recomputes it.** So
`SimSnapshot` carries the boss's committed facing, the declare tick, the
Triple Attack hit ticks, the grapple window bounds, enrage state and the
in-flight input count. If the renderer derived any of these itself, the
picture and the hitbox could drift apart and the sim would start teaching
footwork that does not transfer.

`hazardTiles` is the clearest case: it is the exact `Set` the engine will
test the player's tile against one tick later. The renderer decodes and
draws it. There is no second implementation of the 5×6, the 5×5+3 lines,
or the 9×9 / 11×11 rings.

## Architecture

The loop, camera, interpolation, effect pools, quality tiers and perf
budget all live in the shared
[arena render core](../../arena/README.md) — read that first for the
tick-to-frame model, the tier table and how to add an effect without
allocating. This package supplies only what is specific to the Colosseum.

| File | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `palette.ts` | stone/bronze/gold colours + the Colosseum's gradients |
| `arenaLayer.ts` | the static arena, baked to an offscreen canvas |
| `actors.ts` | Sol, the player, wind-up poses, overhead call-outs |
| `overlay.ts` | sand, hazards, beams, safe rings, markers |
| `renderer.ts` | `ColosseumRenderer` — event→effect and scene assembly |

## Telegraphs

The four wind-ups are deliberately different silhouettes, because that is
the skill being taught:

| Attack | Pose | Hazard |
|---|---|---|
| Spear 1 | weapon raised high and level, arms overhead | 5×6 block + two 4×1 lines |
| Spear 2 | weapon drawn back at the hip, body coiled | 5×5 + three 4×1 lines |
| Shield 1 | shield lifted chest-high | everything but the 9×9 ring |
| Shield 2 | shield lifted overhead, two-handed | everything but the 11×11 ring |

Triple Attack shows one charge pip per incoming swing, filling as its hit
tick approaches — the 3 / 3 / (3 or 4) spacing is visible as the different
fill rate of the third pip, which is exactly what catches people out below
50 %. Grapple drops Sol's shield to the sand and shows the called body
part with one pip per remaining parry tick, the rightmost being the
perfect-parry tick.

## Assists and honest reporting

Anything that makes the fight easier to *play* is off by default and
flagged afterwards:

- The four engine assists (`AssistOptions`: hazard overlay, next-attack
  prediction, prayer-timing indicator, safe-tile highlighting) are
  reported by `ResultsSummary.assistsUsed`, and the canvas shows an
  "N assists on" chip while any is active.
- Two render-side aids — the tile-coordinate grid and the tactical
  view — are listed by `visualAidsInUse()` and shown next to the results.

Some things are deliberately **not** assists, because they exist in game:

- The hazard dust itself. Sol's slams throw visible dust; hiding the
  shape would make the sim teach the wrong thing. The assist adds a
  harder fill, a per-tile outline and the green safe wash.
- The **boundary line** between hazard and safe tiles. It traces only
  the edges where the engine's hazard set meets a safe tile, which for a
  shield slam is exactly the 9×9 / 11×11 corridor — the edge of dust the
  engine already told us about.
- The Triple Attack charge row and the grapple parry pips, which mirror
  animation cadence the player can read anyway. Spelling out the tick
  number (`FLICK IN 2`, `LANDS IN 1`) *is* gated behind an assist.
- Naming the wind-up ("SPEAR 2 — THRUST"), since the four poses are
  already distinct silhouettes. Toggleable via `showAttackLabel`.

## Camera

Fit-to-arena by default. Zoom is 0.7×–1.6× of the fit scale, with an
optional gentle follow. The camera is clamped so the union of the player
and the 11×11 box around Sol — which bounds every safe ring the shield
slams leave — always fits; if the requested zoom cannot show it, the zoom
is reduced until it can. **The camera never hides a hazard the player has
to react to.**

The tactical view flattens to a plain square grid: cheapest to render and
the least ambiguous for pure footwork drilling.

## Tests

`tests/colosseum/render.test.ts` covers the pure parts under vitest's node
environment — no DOM:

- every tile round-trips `projectTile → screenToTile` in both views;
- orientation (north up-screen, east right) and exact `project`/
  `unproject` inversion;
- the camera fits the arena and reduces zoom until the must-see rect fits;
- the effect pool recycles rather than grows, honours the tier cap,
  retires effects beyond it, expires on lifetime, and freezes at `peak`;
- `TileSetCache` decodes the engine's hazard set **tile-for-tile for all
  four AoEs**, compared against `aoeHazardTiles` itself;
- quality tiers are monotonic in cost and `low` really does disable
  everything;
- `visualAidsInUse` reports nothing for default settings.
